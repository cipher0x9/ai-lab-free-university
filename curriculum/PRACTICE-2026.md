# Curriculum practice layer — 2026

This additive layer turns every curriculum topic into a production-minded
experiment without making one model, vendor, country, or job title the center.

## Seven lines every lesson carries

1. Name the domain-specific 2026 practice focus from `taxonomy.py`.
2. Hold the task and evaluator constant while changing one system variable.
3. Record quality, p95 latency, cost per verified task, and a safety signal.
4. Force an empty, malformed, unauthorized, stale, or timeout path.
5. Preserve model, prompt, data, index, tool, and policy versions in Trace.
6. Keep a human owner and approval artifact for material side effects.
7. Retain the last GREEN configuration and prove rollback.

## Production patterns

- **RAG:** evaluate lexical/dense/hybrid retrieval and reranking before answer quality.
- **Evals:** combine deterministic checks, calibrated judges, and named human review.
- **Agents:** trace state, tools, approvals, correction, assertion, cancellation, and stop.
- **Routing:** compare local and frontier models on privacy, pass rate, p95, and verified-task cost.
- **Operations:** canary changes, correlate spans, convert incidents to fixtures, and rehearse rollback.

`generate_lifetime.py` emits this contract into all 425 lifetime lessons.
`generate_resources.py` emits five source-verification checks into every catalog entry.
