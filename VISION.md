# Vision — AI Lab Free University

**Product:** free, offline-friendly AI university for a **general audience**.  
**Bar:** meet or exceed the finish quality of [UC Lab Free University](https://github.com/cipher0x9/uc-lab-free-university) — and beat it on **runnable proof** (labs + evals + RTMA artifacts).

**Author:** CYPHER0X9 · UC domain expert · AI learner-builder  
**Baseline:** 2026-07-31 · expanded general-audience edition  

---

## Universal north star

**AI mastery for everyone with proof.** The campus serves any learner—student,
career-changer, engineer, founder, teacher, operator, artist, researcher, or
domain expert—in any country. It assumes curiosity, not credentials; it teaches
portable mechanisms before vendor menus; and it keeps a zero-key, offline entry
path so money, bandwidth, and account access do not decide who can begin.

Four durable truths organize the program:

1. **Local-first privacy:** data boundaries are designed before model calls.
2. **Frontier capability by evidence:** use stronger remote models when measured value justifies the boundary.
3. **Agentic workflows under control:** tools, permissions, budgets, traces, and human approvals form the system.
4. **Evals as discipline:** versioned tests turn changing models into manageable engineering components.

The target is not dependence on one model. The target is a learner who can
understand, compare, route, evaluate, and safely replace models as the field changes.

See [FUTURE-OF-AI.md](./FUTURE-OF-AI.md) for the 2026–2030 horizon.

## World-class engineering bar

The campus now treats each lesson as a small derivation: invariant first,
provider exception second, worked implementation third, falsifier last. The
public wording stays plain English while the curriculum engine enforces this
order consistently.

By day 90, a learner should be able to show:

1. A local-to-cloud comparison using one adapter contract
2. A RAG ablation with retrieval metrics separate from answer metrics
3. A bounded agent trace with an actual correction and hard stop
4. A calibrated eval set with deterministic, judge, and human layers
5. An optional voice budget split across STT, model/tools, TTS, and transport
6. A stranger-runnable capstone with rollback and RTMA evidence

North-star metric: **cost per verified task**, not prompts sent or demos watched.
See [NEXT-LEVEL-ENGINEERING.md](./NEXT-LEVEL-ENGINEERING.md).

## Future-ready design test

A curriculum change belongs here when it strengthens at least one portable
capability: local execution, multimodal interaction, tool interoperability,
retrieval, bounded agency, evaluation, observability, privacy, or human judgment.
It must still work as a lesson when today’s model name disappears. The release
artifact stays readable offline, the experiment keeps a falsifier, and every
material side effect retains a human decision point.
